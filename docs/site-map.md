# Site map: every player screen and how to get between them

*So what: this page shows where each screen is, how you reach it and where it leads.*
*A clickable version with search and journeys is [site-map.html](site-map.html).*

## The screens
- Each entry says what you do there, how you get there and what comes next.
- The top bar (Courses, World Editor, World Slicer, Community) is the fastest way between the four main places.
- Indented entries live inside the entry above them.

- **Welcome screen**
  - You do: You see the logo, two runners in a park and four big tiles.
  - How to get there: Open the game with no extra words in the address.
  - Leads to: The Course (play), Courses picker, World Editor, World Slicer, Community
- **Top bar**
  - You do: You jump between the four main places from one bar at the top of the page.
  - How to get there: It is on the Course, World Editor, World Slicer, Community and Dojo pages.
  - Leads to: Courses picker, World Editor, World Slicer, Community
- **Courses picker**
  - You do: You pick a course, a lesson or a tool from cards with pictures.
  - How to get there: Press the Courses button in the top bar.
  - Leads to: The Course (play), Parts gallery, Coaster builder, World Editor, World Slicer, Dojo, University Labs
  - **Start here**
    - You do: You find the gentle first course and the harder showcase courses.
    - How to get there: Courses picker, group "Start here".
    - Leads to: The Course (play)
  - **Real places**
    - You do: You run over the roofs of real streets such as Montmartre or Sydney.
    - How to get there: Courses picker, group "Real places".
    - Leads to: The Course (play)
  - **Contraptions**
    - You do: You try a coaster, a trebuchet, a band, a drone and a cannon.
    - How to get there: Courses picker, group "Contraptions".
    - Leads to: The Course (play)
  - **Make your own**
    - You do: You get a random course the simulator has proved clearable, or open the World Slicer, the World Editor or the coaster builder.
    - How to get there: Courses picker, group "Make your own".
    - Leads to: The Course (play), World Slicer, World Editor, Coaster builder
  - **The Making Of**
    - You do: You open the maps of how the game was made, the site map and the course map, as ordinary pages.
    - How to get there: Courses picker, last group "The Making Of" (`?making=0` hides it).
    - Leads to: Courses picker, The Course (play)
- **The Course (play)**
  - You do: You run, jump, hang, swing and climb a small runner to the finish.
  - How to get there: Welcome screen (any key), or pick a course in the Courses picker.
  - Leads to: End report, World Editor, Courses picker, A friend's challenge link
  - **First-visit tour**
    - You do: A short guide shows the camera, the keys, retry and the best line.
    - How to get there: It opens by itself on your first course; the ? button plays it again.
    - Leads to: The Course (play)
  - **Help chip (?)**
    - You do: You see your controls, replay the tour or go to Courses.
    - How to get there: Round ? button, bottom left of any course.
    - Leads to: Courses picker
  - **Characters picker**
    - You do: You change who runs: a Rookie, a starter character or one you made.
    - How to get there: Characters button at the top of the course.
    - Leads to: Community, The Course (play)
  - **Ghost chip**
    - You do: You race a see-through runner: the best line, or a friend.
    - How to get there: Ghost chip at the top, or press N.
    - Leads to: The Course (play)
  - **Fall panel**
    - You do: You see where you fell and can retry, edit the course or save a clip.
    - How to get there: It appears when you fall; press R to retry at once.
    - Leads to: The Course (play), World Editor
  - **End report**
    - You do: You see your time, publish it, copy a link and save a video.
    - How to get there: Reach the finish line.
    - Leads to: Leaderboard, Share a run, World Editor, The Course (play)
    - **Leaderboard**
      - You do: You see the best times on this course and where you stand.
      - How to get there: Press "Publish to Leaderboard" in the end report, or look at the board in the corner.
      - Leads to: The Course (play)
    - **Share a run**
      - You do: You copy a short link, or save a video, so a friend can chase you.
      - How to get there: End report: "Copy link" or "Download video".
      - Leads to: A friend's challenge link
- **A friend's challenge link**
  - You do: You open a short link and the course plays your friend's run as a ghost.
  - How to get there: Open a link that someone copied from their end report.
  - Leads to: The Course (play)
- **Parts gallery**
  - You do: You see every move and every obstacle on one page, each playing live.
  - How to get there: Courses picker, card "Parts: moves and obstacles".
  - Leads to: The Course (play)
- **Coaster builder**
  - You do: You drag dots to shape a track and then ride it.
  - How to get there: Courses picker, group "Make your own", card "Build your own coaster".
  - Leads to: The Course (play)
- **World Editor**
  - You do: You place real objects and bars into a lane and the simulator checks it can be cleared.
  - How to get there: Top bar: World Editor.
  - In a course: "Edit this course".
  - Leads to: The Course (play), Community, World Slicer
  - **Add pieces**
    - You do: You pick shapes, real objects, bars, machines or your own pieces.
    - How to get there: World Editor, "Add pieces" button.
    - Leads to: World Editor
  - **Fix menu**
    - You do: The simulator says in plain words why a lane cannot be cleared and shows a small change.
    - How to get there: World Editor, "Fix" button at the top.
    - Leads to: World Editor
  - **Moves and Design partner**
    - You do: You see which moves your lane has and ask a helper what to change.
    - How to get there: World Editor, "Moves" and "Design partner" buttons.
    - Leads to: World Editor
  - **Play this lane**
    - You do: You run the lane you built.
    - How to get there: World Editor, right column, "Play this lane".
    - Leads to: The Course (play)
- **World Slicer**
  - You do: You pick a real place and cut a lane out of its buildings.
  - How to get there: Top bar: World Slicer.
  - Leads to: The Course (play), World Editor
  - **Pick a place**
    - You do: You search for a place or choose a ready one, with street photos if it has them.
    - How to get there: World Slicer, search box or place cards.
    - Leads to: Cut and slice
  - **Cut and slice**
    - You do: You choose the height line and the buildings, then slice the place.
    - How to get there: World Slicer, "Slice this place".
    - Leads to: Build this place
  - **Build this place**
    - You do: The place becomes a course you can run.
    - How to get there: World Slicer, "Build this place".
    - Leads to: The Course (play), World Editor
- **Community**
  - You do: You turn a photo into a 3D piece and see pieces other people made.
  - How to get there: Top bar: Community.
  - Leads to: World Editor
  - **Explore**
    - You do: You look at pieces people made and add one to your editor.
    - How to get there: Community opens here first.
    - Leads to: Make, World Editor
  - **Make**
    - You do: You pick a photo, click the thing you want, and make a 3D piece.
    - How to get there: Explore, button "Make your own".
    - Leads to: Prize screen
  - **Prize screen**
    - You do: Your new piece spins on a turntable and you can keep it, share it or use it.
    - How to get there: It opens when a piece is ready.
    - Leads to: World Editor, Explore
- **Dojo**
  - You do: You learn one bit of physics at a time by trying, guessing and testing.
  - How to get there: Courses picker, group "Dojo (learn the physics, then play)".
  - Leads to: The Course (play), Courses picker
  - **A Dojo lesson**
    - You do: You move the knobs, guess the result, watch the test and then see the shape of the rule.
    - How to get there: Pick a lesson card, such as "Gap jump" or "Rope swing".
    - Leads to: The Course (play)
- **University Labs**
  - You do: You explore twelve bigger ideas, such as search or camera maths, and equations are allowed here.
  - How to get there: Courses picker, group "Labs (university level)".
  - Leads to: Courses picker
  - **A Lab**
    - You do: You change settings and read the numbers in one hands-on page.
    - How to get there: Pick a Lab card; the Labs bar at the top switches Labs.
    - Leads to: University Labs
- **Feedback card**
  - You do: You tell the team what was fun or confusing in a short form.
  - How to get there: The small Feedback button on every screen.
  - Leads to: back to where you were

## Journeys
- Each journey is a path through the screens above.

### Play your first course
1. Welcome screen
   - Open the game and press any key.
2. First-visit tour
   - Read the short tour (Next, or Skip).
3. The Course (play)
   - Run with the arrow keys and jump with Space.
4. Fall panel
   - Fell? Press R and try again.
5. End report
   - Reach the finish and read your time.
6. Courses picker
   - Press Courses to pick the next one.

### Make your own course
1. Courses picker
   - Press Courses, or go straight to the top bar.
2. World Editor
   - Press World Editor.
3. Add pieces
   - Press "Add pieces" and place a bench, a wall or a bar.
4. Fix menu
   - Press Fix to see if it can be cleared and what to change.
5. Play this lane
   - Press "Play this lane".
6. The Course (play)
   - Run your own course.

### Run over a real place
1. Top bar
   - Use the top bar.
2. World Slicer
   - Press World Slicer.
3. Pick a place
   - Search for a place, or choose a ready one.
4. Cut and slice
   - Choose the height and buildings, then press "Slice this place".
5. Build this place
   - Press "Build this place".
6. The Course (play)
   - Run the lane you cut.

### Learn the physics in the Dojo
1. Courses picker
   - Press Courses.
2. Dojo
   - Scroll to the Dojo group.
3. A Dojo lesson
   - Pick a lesson, move the knobs, guess, then test.
4. The Course (play)
   - Go back to a course and use what you learned.

### Share a run
1. The Course (play)
   - Clear a course.
2. End report
   - Open the end report.
3. Leaderboard
   - Optionally type a three-letter tag and publish to the leaderboard.
4. Share a run
   - Press "Copy link" or "Download video".
5. A friend's challenge link
   - Your friend opens the link and chases your ghost.

### Turn a photo into a jump
1. Top bar
   - Use the top bar.
2. Community
   - Press Community.
3. Explore
   - Look at what others made.
4. Make
   - Press "Make your own", pick a photo and click the thing you want.
5. Prize screen
   - Wait for the spinning prize screen.
6. World Editor
   - Press "Open in the World Editor" and place your piece.
7. The Course (play)
   - Press "Play this lane" and jump over it.

### The refactor safety net
- Run these before and after any move of code; they must give the same answers.
- `tests/sim-trace.test.ts` with `tests/golden/sim-trace.json`: the physics, every tick.
- `tests/golden/course-hashes.json`: the fingerprint of each listed course.
- `tests/golden-search.test.ts`: the ideal-run search keeps finding the same lines.
- `tests/replay.test.ts`: a recorded run replays to the same result.
- `scripts/editor-characterize.mjs`: coming, it will exist after the editor refactor branch ships.
- The split of `src/modes/course.ts` and `src/modes/editor.ts` is described in `docs/refactor-review.md`.

### How to check a change
- Any change: `npm run test:quick`.
- Physics or a move (`src/sim/`): the safety net above first, then `tests/sim.test.ts` and the test named after the move.
- Course screen (`src/modes/course.ts`, `src/modes/course/`): `tests/course.test.ts`, `tests/course-keydown.test.ts`, then browser checks `smoke`, `drums`, `event-key`, `restart`, `shortcut`.
- End report, leaderboard, share or ghost: the unit tests for that row, then browser checks `report` and `board-best`; the red scripts in that row do not count as passing.
- World Editor: the editor unit tests, then browser checks `editor-flow`, `editor-select`, `editor-defaults`, `real-objects`; run `scripts/editor-characterize.mjs` once it exists.
- World Slicer, Community, Dojo, Labs: the unit tests in that row, then its browser checks; none has a recorded status.
- A doc change: `node scripts/doc-style.mjs`, `node scripts/check-personal.mjs`, `npx vitest run tests/doc-links.test.ts`.
- A UI or motion change also needs a screenshot or clip under `test-results/`.
