# CC-135: a refused challenge link offers the ideal ghost

- Serves the Most Viral prize: a friend who opens an old link still gets something to chase.
- When the course changed, the build is older or the keys do not clear, the banner says so and has a button "Chase the ideal run instead".
- The button turns the ideal ghost on and closes the banner.
- A damaged link offers nothing, because the course itself may not be the one meant.
- The decision is one small function, `offersIdeal`, with `tests/share-offer.test.ts`.
- Checked by `scripts/share-offer-check.mjs` (6 of 6 PASS).
- Screenshots: `offer.png`, `after.png`.
